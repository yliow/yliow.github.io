Campus track

The campus track will be held at Columbia College, time and details are listed below. Participants will get to know current CCCS students and chat about computer science or anything else during the warm-up phase of the contest.

If you DON'T have a team but are looking for one, we can help you find a team during our morning warmup session.

  • You can choose to be a team of one.
  • You need not be registered by your teacher -- you can register individually.
  • You need not arrive on our campus in a school bus.
  • You or your parents can drive to the event too, parking is free.
  • Parents and teachers are welcome. (Please limit guests to 2)
  • Food is provided for parents and teachers too!

What’s the schedule? [goto top]

The event begins at 9AM CST (15:00 UTC). The contest itself begins after lunch. It's OK to check in late especially if you are driving from far -- just let us know.
  • 8AM-9AM: CHECK-IN, BREAKFAST, OPENING -- DULANY HALL (BANQUET ROOM)
    • Panera breakfast. (Drinks and snacks provided throughout the day.)
    • Opening remarks at ~8:45AM
  • 9AM-11:50AM: WARMUPS -- BUCHANAN HALL
    • Practice session.
    • Decide which category you want to join. We'll help you decide if you need help.
    • Form teams. We'll also help to form teams if you don't have one. It's OK if you prefer to work alone. But it's more fun to work in a group.
  • 12PM-12:40PM: LUNCH -- DULANY HALL (BANQUET ROOM)
    • Buffet lunch
    • Talk and Q&A with CCCS graduate Michael Fisher.

      Michael Fisher graduated from CCCS in 2022. Michael Fisher is an embedded software engineer. He worked at Indra Air Traffic, part of Indra Sistemas -- one of the world's top three companies in air traffic management (ATM) and a leading Spanish multinational specializing in defense, aerospace, and digital transformation. In January 2025, Michael joined Garmin, the undisputed #1 leader in the world in general aviation avionics and GPS navigation systems.

  • 12:45PM-5PM: CONTEST -- BUCHANAN HALL
  • 5PM: DINNER, CLOSING -- DULANY HALL (BANQUET ROOM)
    • Prize presentation
    • Closing remarks
    • Domino's pizzas
During the contest, depending on interest from the guests, there might be information and discussion sessions for parents and teachers.

Where do I register? [goto top]

  • Registration opens: 10/02/2026
  • Registration closes: 2/25/2027. We will try to accomodate late registrations. Go ahead and register!
  • Students: registration form.
    • Parents/guardians/siblings are welcome and encouraged to attend and can register as guests when a student registers.
  • Teachers: registration form.
    • Teachers are welcome and encouraged to register and attend the event. Even if you are not attending the event, you can enter your contact info so that we can email you info on our future events.
    • If you are a teacher registering a group of students, you can either ask your students to register individually or you can email Dr. Liow (yliow@ccis.edu).
  • Thanks to our generous sponsors, this contest is 100% free and includes complimentary meals and scholarship opportunities. Because we order food and allocate prizes based on registration numbers, every empty seat represents wasted college resources. If your plans change, please cancel your registration ASAP (email Dr. Liow at yliow@ccis.edu) so we can give your seat (and meals!) to a student on the waitlist.
